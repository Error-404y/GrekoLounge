Need to know

Frequently asked

Is my order confirmed immediately?+

No. Submission creates a pending request. Our team confirms availability and next steps.

Why is face value different from price?+

Face value is the card amount; purchase price is what the listing costs. Both are shown clearly.

What is the minimum request?+

The current minimum is €100. Your cart shows progress toward it.

What should I keep after ordering?+

Save the unique order reference shown after submission so support can locate it.
